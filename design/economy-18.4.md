# Economy after 18.4.0

Model from tools/balance/econ.js: for each level, the average monster that spawns at that level (no NMs or bosses), solo even fights with time to kill and HP lost from the 18.3 simulator, plus resting back to full and 20 s to find the next target. Income = crystals dropped + materials sold (a quarter of price) + gear drops sold (6% chance). Full set = best shop weapon plus head, body, hands, legs, feet, neck, waist, back, two ears and two rings for a median job (WAR, WHM, BLM, THF, PLD, MNK, RDM, RNG). Quest rewards, crafting, fishing and guild points are not counted.
